About us

ALPHADEK Instruments designs and manufactures instrumentation for defence systems. Our name comes from our symbol: two stars and a spark, the precise point a good instrument knows how to find.

Mission

Instruments you can trust

Whoever uses our systems must be able to rely on them in the toughest conditions. That is why we design from real-world use and test every instrument before it leaves our bench.

01

Precision

Reliable, repeatable measurements, verified with documented test procedures.

02

Reliability

Components selected and qualified to last, with simple and predictable maintenance.

03

Responsibility

We operate in compliance with export regulations and the controls that apply to defence materiel.
