Systems

Product lines

Four families of systems for targeting, observation, navigation and maintenance. Each line can be supplied on its own or integrated with the others.

LINE ADK-FCFC

Fire control

Computers and interfaces that collect data from on-board sensors and present it to the crew clearly and immediately. Designed both for new platforms and for upgrading existing ones.

  • Real-time processing of sensor data
  • Standard interfaces to on-board systems
  • Crew displays and controls
  • Modular, upgradable architecture
LINE ADK-EOEO

Optronics

Electro-optical sights and sensors for observation, surveillance and targeting, with day and thermal channels and video outputs to on-board systems.

  • Day channel and thermal channel
  • Image stabilisation
  • Optional laser rangefinder
  • Standard video outputs
LINE ADK-NAVNAV

Navigation

Reference units that provide position and attitude to vehicles and systems, keeping their orientation even when no satellite signal is available.

  • Inertial position and attitude reference
  • Operation without a satellite signal
  • Fast alignment at start-up
  • Outputs to targeting and navigation systems
LINE ADK-CALCAL

Test and calibration

Benches and instruments to test and calibrate systems during maintenance, with guided procedures and recording of the results.

  • Test benches for 1st and 2nd level maintenance
  • Guided calibration procedures
  • Recording of test results
  • Laboratory and field versions

Detailed datasheets are available on request. Contact us stating the platform and intended use.
